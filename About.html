About Me

My name is Simon Gerhardt Rassing.

I am a young man with loads of ambition of being a big part in the creative industry. Movies, comics, novels, animated shows, you name it. I wanna create many deep worlds, characters and compelling stories.

I was born and raised in Denmark. At a young age I was diagnosed with type 1 diabetes, i was always easily sick, a couple years after being diagnosed with diabetes i was diagnosed with celiac disease. Still i was always tired. I dont have many memories of my early childhood. Turned out, we were still missing one diagnosis, i was diagnosed at the age of 19 with addisons decease. An illness that made my body unable to regulate stress hormones normally.

Now, hopefully, that is it for the deceases. Because through all of it, my imagination was still as lively as ever, and i have loved making up stories the entire time. During public school i was also enrolled in a Next film school for a year. After public school i went to a media school and am currently in a media focused academia.
